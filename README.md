<h1 align="center">🤖 BotMaker – Telegram Bot Builder</h1>

<p align="center">
  <i>Create your own Telegram bot without writing a single line of code!</i>

---

<h2>🚀 What is BotMaker?</h2>
<p>
  <b>BotMaker</b> is a powerful platform that lets you design and launch 
  <b>Telegram bots</b> with <span style="color:#4CAF50">zero coding knowledge</span>.  
  Whether you need a bot for business, automation, or fun – BotMaker makes it as 
  simple as drag, drop, and deploy.
</p>

---

<h2>✨ Features</h2>
<ul>
  <li>📦 <b>No-Code Bot Creation</b> – Build bots with a simple interface</li>
  <li>⚡ <b>Instant Deployment</b> – Go live on Telegram in seconds</li>
  <li>🎨 <b>Customizable</b> – Add buttons, menus, and responses easily</li>
  <li>🛠️ <b>Multi-Function Support</b> – From auto-replies to advanced workflows</li>
  <li>🌍 <b>Cross-Platform</b> – Works anywhere, anytime</li>
</ul>



<h2>🛠️ How It Works</h2>

<ol>
  <li>Clone this repo:  
    <pre><code>git clone https://github.com/Salar-pr/BotMaker-.git</code></pre>
  </li>
  <li>Install requirements:  
    <pre><code>pip install -r requirements.txt</code></pre>
  </li>
  <li>Run the platform:  
    <pre><code>python app.py</code></pre>
  </li>
  <li>Start building your bot 🎉</li>
</ol>
